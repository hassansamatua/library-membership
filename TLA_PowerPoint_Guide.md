3. **Colors:** 
   - Primary: TLA Green (#10b981)
   - Secondary: Dark gray (#2d3e50)
   - Accent: Light gray (#f3f4f6)
   - Text: Black for headers, dark gray for body

### Visual Elements:
1. **Logo:** Place TLA logo on title slide and footer of all slides
2. **Images:** Include screenshots of the actual card interface
3. **Diagrams:** Use clean, professional diagrams for architecture and flows
4. **Icons:** Use consistent icon style throughout
